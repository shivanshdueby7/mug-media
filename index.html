01 — Why it works

Pretty is easy.
Clear is the hard part.

Attention is short. Trust is earned in seconds. These are the four decisions we make on every site — before a single pixel gets pretty.

The part most redesigns skip.

01

One job per screen.

Every section asks for one thing. When everything shouts, nothing lands.

02

Proof before promises.

Real work, real names, real links. A claim is cheap; evidence does the selling.

03

Answer the quiet doubt.

“Is this for me? What does it cost? What happens next?” We answer it before they have to ask.

04

Make the next step obvious.

One primary action, repeated at the right moments. Not five buttons fighting for the same thumb.

The 5-second test

Show a stranger your homepage for five seconds. Look away. Ask what you do. If they can’t answer, the page is leaking trust — and money.

05 — FAQ

The questions you’d ask on the call.

Still not sure? Email us. Worst case, you get a useful answer.

support.mugmedia@gmail.com No hard sell. Promise.
How much will it cost?

Scope decides. A one-page launch and a full multi-page site aren’t the same job. Tell us what you need and you’ll get a fixed quote in writing — not an hourly meter.

How long does it take?

The timeline is agreed before we start and depends on scope, content, and how quickly feedback comes back. We only take on what we can finish.

Do you write the copy?

Yes. We’ll interview you, pull out the useful bits, and write it so a stranger understands why you’re worth choosing. You approve every word.

Can you fix my existing site?

Usually. Send the link and tell us what isn’t working. If a rebuild makes more sense, we’ll say so — even when the smaller job would be easier for us.

What do you need from me?

A conversation, your logo and photos if you have them, and honest answers. If the content isn’t ready, we’ll help shape it.

What happens after launch?

You get the keys and a walkthrough. If you want ongoing updates, new pages, or a care plan, we can stay on. No lock-in.

Do you only work with big budgets?

No. We work with small brands and first launches. If it isn’t a fit, we’ll tell you early and point you somewhere useful.
